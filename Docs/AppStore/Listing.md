## Description (4,000 characters max)

```
Roadivo turns road trips into movie nights. Play your own IPTV playlists, media servers and videos on your iPhone, on your car's CarPlay screen while you're parked, or over AirPlay to a car display. See your routes in Maps, and get big, easy controls with Car Mode on a dashboard mount.

BRING YOUR OWN CONTENT
• Add an M3U or M3U8 playlist from a link, a file, pasted text or a QR code, or paste a single stream link
• Sign in to your own Jellyfin, Emby, Plex or Xtream server
• Import videos from Photos or Files and watch them offline
• Browse by group, search by name or by voice, and keep favorites

ON THE CAR'S SCREEN
• Roadivo on CarPlay puts your Home, Favorites and Library on the car's screen. Video shows there only while the car is parked; while driving, the sound plays on
• Play on Car Display and Play on iPhone move a video between the two screens
• Cast to Screen shows your iPhone's screen on the car's display while parked
• AirPlay to a car display, wireless adapter or TV

MADE FOR THE ROAD
• Car Mode: large landscape controls and a clear progress bar for an iPhone on a dashboard mount. It can open by itself when your car connects
• Drive View hides the video and keeps three big buttons for the driver
• Car & Display Profiles remember the aspect ratio, subtitles and playback settings for each car or screen
• Picture in Picture keeps playing while you use other apps
• Responds to steering-wheel and headphone media buttons

MAPS
• Search for a place and see the driving routes from where you are, with travel time, distance and arrival time
• Pick a route and fold its card away to see the whole map
• A small map stays in the corner while you go back to your videos
• Roadivo shows routes; it doesn't give turn-by-turn directions

DISCOVER
• A built-in browser opens popular video websites, and a direct video link plays in Roadivo's player

PICK UP WHERE YOU LEFT OFF
• Continue Watching resumes films and episodes where you stopped
• Up Next counts down to the next video, with Play Now and Cancel
• Source Health checks which streams work before you set off

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No ads, no tracking, no account
• Your playlists, logins and history stay on your iPhone
• Location is used only for routes in Maps and to tell when the car is parked, and is never stored
• Themes, accent colors, fonts and 11 languages

ROADIVO PRO
```
