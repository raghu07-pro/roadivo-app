PlayCarTV Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

PlayCarTV is a media player. You bring your own playlists, media servers and videos. PlayCarTV plays them. This policy explains what happens to your data.

1The short version

  • Your playlists, media servers, sign-in details and videos stay on your iPhone. We don't record what you watch.
  • PlayCarTV has no analytics, no ads and no tracking. It never asks to track you.
  • There are no accounts, and we never sell or share personal information.

2Data stored on your iPhone

  • Playlist links, Xtream logins and media-server access tokens are kept in the iOS Keychain.
  • Channel lists, favourites and watch history are kept in the app's private storage. So are stream addresses, which often include your login or access token.
  • Videos you import from Photos or Files are copied into the app and deleted when you remove them.
  • Car & Display Profiles keep the names of the AirPlay screens, CarPlay cars and displays you save, with your settings for each.
  • The Browser keeps no history or bookmarks. Websites you visit can keep cookies and other data on your iPhone, as in any browser.

3Network connections

  • PlayCarTV connects only to the playlist links, IPTV providers and media servers you add, and to the stream addresses they list, including when you check a source in Source Health. Those services have their own privacy policies.
  • In the Browser, PlayCarTV connects to the websites you open, and words you search for go to Google. Those sites' own privacy policies apply. PlayCarTV keeps no record of what you do there.
  • The icons on the Browser's start page come from Google's icon service, which is told only the names of those sites. They're kept on your iPhone and fetched again about once a month.
  • Once a day PlayCarTV asks Apple's App Store whether a newer version is out, sending only the app's ID and your country.
  • To connect Plex, PlayCarTV signs you in at plex.tv with a code and asks Plex for your list of servers. Plex's privacy policy applies to your Plex account.
  • Media servers you connect, including Plex, see a random identifier for this copy of PlayCarTV so they can list it among your devices. It isn't linked to you anywhere else.
  • AirPlay sends video only to the screen you pick.
  • Screen Broadcast (Discover › Cast to Screen) shows your iPhone's screen on PlayCarTV's screen in your car through CarPlay, only while you broadcast and the car is parked. The picture passes from PlayCarTV's broadcast extension to PlayCarTV on the same iPhone and never leaves it: nothing is recorded, saved or sent anywhere.
  • On CarPlay, what PlayCarTV shows on your car's screen comes from your iPhone. PlayCarTV sends nothing about you or your car anywhere.

4Camera and Photos

  • The camera is used only while you scan a QR code, and nothing is recorded.
  • PlayCarTV sees only the photos and videos you pick.

5Microphone and speech

  • The microphone is used only while you search by voice, and nothing is recorded or kept. Speech becomes text on your iPhone where your language allows it, otherwise through Apple's speech service.

6Location

  • PlayCarTV asks for your location (While Using the App) when you open Maps, to show driving routes from where you are, and when it's open on CarPlay, to tell whether the car is parked: video plays on the car's screen only then, and sound alone while the car moves.
  • Readings are used as they arrive, on your iPhone. PlayCarTV doesn't store them, link them to you or send them to us.
  • Maps finds places and routes with Apple Maps: what you search for and your location go to Apple for that, under Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Without location, Maps can't show routes from where you are, and PlayCarTV on CarPlay plays sound only. You can turn it off at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services.

7Purchases

  • PlayCarTV Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple handles payment; we never see your card or Apple Account details.

8Children

  • PlayCarTV isn't directed at children under 13, and it collects no personal information from anyone.

9Your rights

  • PlayCarTV keeps nothing about you on our side, so there's nothing for us to access, correct or delete. Everything stays on your iPhone, under your control.

10Deleting your data

  • Delete a source in Settings › Manage Sources to remove it and its login. To remove everything, delete your sources, then delete the app.

11Changes to this policy

  • If this policy changes, the new version appears in the app and on our website with a new date.

12Contact

  • Questions about this policy? Email dhavalbhanderi880@gmail.com, or reach us through PlayCarTV's App Store page.

Support & FAQ

Tap a question to see the answer

What should I do if something goes wrong while using the app?
Close PlayCarTV from the app switcher and open it again. If a channel still won't play, refresh its source in Settings › Manage Sources. Still stuck? Tap Contact Support at the end of Help & FAQ, or write to the address below.
I bought PlayCarTV Pro, but it still says I'm not a member.
Open the Pro screen and tap Restore. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with. Purchases waiting for Ask to Buy approval unlock once approved.
What is a source? Does PlayCarTV come with channels?
A source is a playlist link or a media server you own or subscribe to. PlayCarTV is a player only: it doesn't provide, host or recommend any content.
Where do I find a source?
From an IPTV service you subscribe to (they send an M3U link, a QR code or Xtream login details), or from your own Plex, Jellyfin or Emby server. Only add sources you have the right to watch.
I pasted a link and it says the download failed or it isn't a valid channel list.
Check the link opens in Safari, starts with http:// or https://, and that your subscription is active. Some providers limit how many devices can use one link.
My source was added, so why does Home show only some of the channels?
Each group shows its first 20 channels in a row. Tap the group's name for all of them, or use search.
What do I need to watch in the car?
A car display or wireless adapter that accepts AirPlay video. Start a channel, then pick the display for AirPlay in Control Center. For a mounted iPhone, tap the car button in the player for Car Mode. Never watch video while driving.
Does casting need Wi‑Fi?
AirPlay needs your iPhone and the screen on the same network, or a direct connection from an AirPlay adapter. Streams also need mobile data or Wi‑Fi; videos on this iPhone play offline.
Why won't a channel play?
Streams go offline, change address or use a format iPhone can't play. Plex, Jellyfin and Emby convert videos automatically; for IPTV, ask your provider for HLS (m3u8) links.
Can I keep watching while I browse?
Yes. Tap the down arrow to minimize the player and it keeps going in the mini player, or swipe home for Picture in Picture.
Is my data private?
Everything you add stays on this iPhone: logins in the Keychain, channel lists in the app's private storage. PlayCarTV has no account, no ads and no tracking.
